About Us

We are a team of year twelve students who have interest in computer science and enjoy creating projects with our computing skills

Our Mission

We aim to maintain health condition of the astronauts in space facilities to create safe and stable environments

By creating an app which is dedicated to health and safety, we take in the values that the astronauts provided and output their health conditions

What We Do

  • Each astronaut could create their own instance on the app using a username and password, and could log on onto the app using it
  • They wear a wristband which is similar to a smartwatch on Earth which could record the health values that we have to monitor. The astronauts will then manually input the values intp the app
  • Using the values, the app outputs if their health values and suggestions on ways to improve specific health values
  • An exercise plan is also suggested to astronauts depending on their situation
  • We have tests for values that cannot be measured like reaction time and a mental health evaluation test which would contribute toi their mental health score
