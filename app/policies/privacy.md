# Privacy Policy 
Effective 15th November 2026

----

At 11:56 by Astronomads, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your data. This privacy policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services.

## Information We Collect
During signup, we ask for your username, creation of a password, biological sex, height(cm), mass(kg), birthday (DD/MM/YYYY) and other information you provide.
## How We Use Your Information
We use personal information to help you analyze your medical health in order to provide you with recommendations to improve your health.

Flight surgeons on your mission can view your profile, the health data you log and your reaction test results, including any readings flagged as outside the normal range, so that they can monitor crew health. They cannot see your password. Flight surgeons can also send you treatment messages; these messages and your replies are stored so that you and the flight surgeons can read them.
## Data Security
We deploy reasonable measures to protect your data at transit and at rest. However, please note that although your data is encrypted, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.
## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date will be listed on this page.
